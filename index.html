Gluten-Free Kringle Recipe

*(Originally from http://www.gfandme.com/2014/04/22/danish-pastry-just-like-grandma-made-only-gluten-free/)*

(2019 Notes: Heidi said too much cherry. Cut in half? Likes lots of cream cheese though. The fillings could probably make 2 Kringles, but that’s too much Kringle for 2 people. We ate most of 1 in a single day, with small pieces saved for the next morning. Heidi also likes the breast edges best)
  • ½ tablespoon yeast
  • ¼ cup warm water
  • ½ teaspoon sugar
  • 1 ¾ cups Cup-4-Cup all-purpose gluten free flour (e.g. Manini's)
  • ¼ cup almond flour
  • Pinch salt
  • ½ cup and 2 tablespoons hard margarine or cold butter
  • egg yolks (reserve some of the whites for an egg wash)
  • tablespoons sour cream
  • Sprinkle of sugar for garnish
  • Cherry Pie Filling and Cream Cheese Filling
  1. In a medium sized bowl, dissolve sugar in water and add yeast to activate it.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the egg yolks and the sour cream.
  3. In your food processor, add flours and salt (and xanthan gum if needed. Cup 4 cup has it included, but if you use a flour mix that doesn’t be sure to add a teaspoon of it) and pulse to combine. Cut the hard margarine or butter (we used margarine in this recipe) into cubes and sprinkle them on top of the flour. Pulse the mixture about 10 times until it is crumbly.
  4. Pour the yolk mixture into the yeast mixture and stir together (the yeast should activate for at least 5 minutes before adding the yolks.)
  5. Now pour the liquid over the flour mixture and pulse (or blend by hand) just until the dough comes together.
  6. Remove the dough from the processor, shape into a ball on parchment, and knead it until it comes together nicely – about 6 or 7 times.
  7. Cover with another piece of parchment and roll out into a rectangle approximately 10 x 12 inches. If necessary, chill a few minutes in freezer to remove stickiness and keep butter from melting, but not too long else the dough will crack when folding around filling.
  8. Remove the top parchment and add filling by spooning a 4 inch strip of filling across the middle of the rectangle from 10 inch side to 10 inch side. Leave about 3 inches free of filling on the top and bottom and about 2 inches on the ends.

    The bottom 2in strip should be cream cheese, and the top 2in strip cherry filling, so that the cherry is folded over the cream cheese in subsequent step.
  9. Fold the sides over the filling, then fold the top down to meet the bottom, bringing the cherry atop the cream cheese. Where the top and bottom meet, seal the dough with water. If they don’t, don’t worry. The dough will open up to reveal the filling inside as it bakes. Beautiful!

    TIP: If you are particularly crafty, or brave, you can cut the 3 inch top and bottom pieces into strips with a sharp knife and fold them over each other to look like a braid.
  10. Move the pastry onto a parchment lined baking sheet.

    TIP: Use the parchment the pastry is sitting on and just move the entire thing over.
  11. ake some of the left over egg whites and beat lightly. Using a pastry brush, brush some of the whites over the top of the pastry and then sprinkle the entire kringle with white sugar.
  12. If desired, can be frozen at this point. *****Before baking the frozen kringle, move to refrigerator in evening to defrost****, then in the morning, remove from fridge and let come to room temp before baking.
  13. Bake at 350 degrees for 35 – 40 minutes or until golden brown in color, **rotating halfway through (super important in camp oven).**
  14. Remove and cool.

To make Glaze:

  • 3/4 cup icing sugar
  • 1 teaspoon soft margarine or butter
  • 1/8 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons milk or cream (may need 1/4 cup or more when using cream)

    Mix the icing sugar, margarine, vanilla and 1 tablespoon of cream or milk together until they form an icing. Add more milk by the teaspoonful until you get the consistency you want.

    While pastry is still warm, brush the glaze onto the kringle.

    Let the glaze harden and serve.
